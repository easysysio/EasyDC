Every FSMO role does not hold yet moves to it, one at a time. Do this before taking another DC out of service.

Remove

For a DC that is gone for good: its server died, or it was switched off without being demoted. Everything it left in the directory and in DNS is deleted, so the other DCs stop trying to replicate with it and clients stop being sent to it. A DC that is still running is demoted on itself instead, with samba-tool domain demote.
